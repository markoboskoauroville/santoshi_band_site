    Songs · our repertoirePjesme · naš repertoar

    The mantras we singMantre koje pjevamo

    Kirtan is singing together: one voice calls a line, everyone answers. The words are mantras, names of the divine in Sanskrit, repeated until the mind goes quiet and the heart opens. You do not need to know the words to join; after two rounds you will be singing along.Kirtan je zajedničko pjevanje: jedan glas zapjeva stih, svi odgovaraju. Riječi su mantre, imena božanskog na sanskrtu, koje se ponavljaju dok se um ne umiri i srce ne otvori. Ne morate znati riječi da biste se pridružili; nakon dva kruga pjevat ćete s nama.

    Navaratri, the nine nights, is the festival of the Goddess. Traditionally the first three nights belong to Durga and Kali, who take away fear, the next three to Lakshmi, who brings abundance, and the last three to Sarasvati, who brings wisdom and music. Our concert of 17 October 2026 followed that path, after first greeting Ganesha.Navaratri, devet noći, blagdan je Božice. Po tradiciji prve tri noći pripadaju Durgi i Kali, koje odnose strah, sljedeće tri Lakšmi, koja donosi obilje, a posljednje tri Sarasvati, koja donosi mudrost i glazbu. Naš koncert 17. listopada 2026. išao je tim putem, nakon što smo najprije pozdravili Ganešu.

    Jai Ganesha Pahi Mam

    toza GaneshaGanešu

    Jaya Gaṇeśa, Gaṇeśa, pāhi mām
    Śrī Gaṇeśa, Gaṇeśa, rakṣa mām
    Gaṃ Gaṇapataye namo namaḥ

    “Victory to Ganesha, protect me; holy Ganesha, guard me. Gam: to Ganapati, salutations again and again.” Ganesha, with the elephant head, removes obstacles and opens every beginning, so a kirtan starts with him.„Slava Ganeši, zaštiti me; sveti Ganeša, čuvaj me. Gam: Ganapatiju, poklon i opet poklon.“ Ganeša sa slonovskom glavom uklanja prepreke i otvara svaki početak, pa kirtan počinje s njim.

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (9)
    jaya
    Victory, triumph. As a call it means “glory to …!”, “victory to …!”. From the root ji, “to conquer”. It shortens to jai in Hindi, as in the song's title.Pobjeda, trijumf. Kao zaziv znači „slava …!”, „pobjeda …!”. Od korijena ji, „pobijediti”. U hindskom se skraćuje u jai, kao u naslovu pjesme.
    Gaṇeśa
    Gaṇa, “the host, the crowd” (Shiva's attendants), + īśa, “lord”: the Lord of the hosts. The elephant-headed son of Shiva and Parvati, remover of obstacles, honoured first at every beginning.Gaṇa, „mnoštvo, četa” (Šivini pratioci), + īśa, „gospodar”: Gospodar mnoštva. Sin Šive i Parvati sa slonovskom glavom, onaj koji uklanja prepreke; slavi se prvi na svakom početku.
    pāhi
    “Protect!” The imperative of the root pā, “to protect, to keep”.„Zaštiti!” Imperativ korijena pā, „štititi, čuvati”.
    mām
    “Me”: the accusative of aham, “I”. Pāhi mām: “protect me”.„Mene”: akuzativ od aham, „ja”. Pāhi mām: „zaštiti me”.
    Śrī
    Radiance, beauty, good fortune, holiness. Placed before a divine name as a mark of honour (“holy”, “revered”); it is also a name of the goddess Lakshmi.Sjaj, ljepota, blagostanje, svetost. Stavlja se pred božansko ime kao znak poštovanja („sveti”, „časni”); to je i ime božice Lakšmi.
    rakṣa
    “Guard! Preserve!” The imperative of the root rakṣ, “to guard, to save”; rakṣā is “protection”. Rakṣa mām: “guard me”.„Čuvaj! Sačuvaj!” Imperativ korijena rakṣ, „čuvati, spasiti”; rakṣā je „zaštita”. Rakṣa mām: „čuvaj me”.
    Gaṃ
    The seed sound (bīja) of Ganesha: one syllable that holds his whole power, sung before his name.Sjemeni zvuk (bīja) Ganeše: jedan slog koji nosi svu njegovu snagu, pjeva se prije njegova imena.
    Gaṇapataye
    “To Ganapati”: gaṇa, “the host, the band of followers”, + pati, “lord”: Ganesha as lord of all beings; the ending -aye means “to”.„Ganapatiju“: gaṇa, „mnoštvo, družina“, + pati, „gospodar“: Ganeša kao gospodar svih bića; nastavak -aye znači „komu“.
    namo namaḥ
    Namaḥ, “bow, salutation” (literally “not mine”): said twice, “salutations again and again”.Namaḥ, „naklon, pozdrav“ (doslovno „ne moje“): rečeno dvaput, „poklon i opet poklon“.

    Ambe Jagadambe

    toza the Divine MotherBožansku Majku

    Ambe, Jagadambe

    Ambā is “mother”, Jagadambā “mother of the whole world”. The song calls the Goddess whom Navaratri celebrates, the one mother behind all her forms.Ambā znači „majka“, Jagadambā „majka cijelog svijeta“. Pjesma zaziva Božicu koju Navaratri slavi, jednu majku iza svih njezinih oblika.

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (2)
    Ambe
    “O Mother!”: the vocative (the form used to call someone) of Ambā, “mother”. A name of the Goddess, Durga.„O Majko!”: vokativ (oblik za dozivanje) od Ambā, „majka”. Ime Božice, Durge.
    Jagadambe
    Jagat, “the world, all that moves” (from gam, “to go”), + ambā, “mother”: “O Mother of the world!”, in the vocative.Jagat, „svijet, sve što se kreće” (od gam, „ići”), + ambā, „majka”: „O Majko svijeta!”, u vokativu.

    Kali Kali Maha Maya

    toza KaliKali

    A medley: Kali Kali Mahamaya · Sarva Mangala · He Mata Kali · Kali Ma, Durga Ma · Jaya JagadambeSplet: Kali Kali Mahamaya · Sarva Mangala · He Mata Kali · Kali Ma, Durga Ma · Jaya Jagadambe

    Kālī Kālī Mahāmāyā Oṃ
    Bhavānī Śaṅkarī Oṃ
    Devī Devī Girijā, Hara Hara Devī
    Sarva maṅgala māṅgalye śive sarvārtha sādhike
    śaraṇye tryambake gauri nārāyaṇi namo'stu te
    He Mātā Kālī, He Mātā Durgā
    Kālī Durgā namo namaḥ
    Kālī Mā Kālī Mā, Kālī Kālī Kālī Mā
    Durgā Mā Durgā Mā, Durgā Durgā Durgā Mā
    Jaya Jaya Jagadambe, Jagadambe Mā Durgā
    Oṃ Nārāyaṇī Oṃ
    He Mā Durgā

    Kali is the fierce, loving mother who cuts away fear and the small self; Mahāmāyā is her great power that makes and unmakes the world. The verse Sarva Mangala, from the Devī Māhātmya, bows to her: “O auspicious one, source of all good, who fulfils every aim, our refuge, three-eyed Gauri, Narayani, we bow to you.”Kali je žestoka i nježna majka koja odsijeca strah i malo ja; Mahāmāyā je njezina velika moć koja stvara i rastvara svijet. Stih Sarva Mangala iz Devī Māhātmye klanja joj se: „O blagoslovljena, izvore svakog dobra, koja ispunjavaš svaku težnju, naše utočište, trooka Gauri, Narayani, klanjamo ti se.“

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (28)
    Kālī
    “The dark one”, and “she who is time”: kāla means both “black” and “time”. The fierce form of the Goddess who ends what must end.„Tamna”, i „ona koja je vrijeme”: kāla znači i „crn” i „vrijeme”. Silni oblik Božice koja dovršava ono što mora završiti.
    Mahā
    “Great”. Mahā Māyā: “the great Māyā”.„Velik, velika”. Mahā Māyā: „velika Māyā”.
    Māyā
    The power by which the One appears as the many: the Goddess's creative power, also called illusion, because the world is not what it seems.Moć kojom se Jedno pojavljuje kao mnoštvo: stvaralačka moć Božice, zvana i iluzija, jer svijet nije ono što se čini.
    sarva
    “All, every, whole”.„Sve, svaki, cijel”.
    maṅgala
    Auspiciousness, blessing, good fortune; whatever brings well-being.Blagoslov, sreća, ono što donosi dobro i napredak.
    māṅgalye
    Vocative of māṅgalyā, “she who is auspiciousness itself”. Sarva maṅgala māṅgalye: “O blessing of all blessings!”Vokativ od māṅgalyā, „ona koja je sam blagoslov”. Sarva maṅgala māṅgalye: „O blagoslove svih blagoslova!”
    śive
    Vocative of Śivā, “the kind, the benevolent one”, the feminine of Śiva: the Goddess as Shiva's own power.Vokativ od Śivā, „dobrostiva, milostiva”, ženski oblik od Śiva: Božica kao Šivina vlastita moć.
    sarvārtha
    Sarva, “every”, + artha, “aim, purpose, wealth, meaning”: every aim of life.Sarva, „svaki”, + artha, „cilj, svrha, bogatstvo, smisao”: svaki cilj života.
    sādhike
    Vocative of sādhikā, “she who brings to fulfilment” (from sādh, “to accomplish”). Sarvārtha sādhike: “O you who fulfil every aim!”Vokativ od sādhikā, „ona koja dovodi do ispunjenja” (od sādh, „ostvariti”). Sarvārtha sādhike: „O ti koja ispunjavaš svaki cilj!”
    śaraṇye
    Vocative of śaraṇyā, “she who gives refuge” (śaraṇa, “shelter, refuge”).Vokativ od śaraṇyā, „ona koja daje utočište” (śaraṇa, „zaklon, utočište”).
    tryambake
    Tri, “three”, + ambaka, “eye”: “O three-eyed one!”, the eye of wisdom on the forehead, in the vocative.Tri, „tri”, + ambaka, „oko”: „O trooka!”, oko mudrosti na čelu, u vokativu.
    gauri
    Vocative of Gaurī, “the fair, the shining one”: a name of Parvati.Vokativ od Gaurī, „svijetla, blistava”: ime Parvati.
    nārāyaṇi
    Vocative of Nārāyaṇī, the power (śakti) of Nārāyaṇa, that is, of Vishnu.Vokativ od Nārāyaṇī, moć (śakti) Nārāyaṇe, to jest Višnua.
    namo'stu
    Namaḥ, “bowing, salutation”, + astu, “let it be” (from as, “to be”): “salutation be (to you)”.Namaḥ, „naklon, pozdrav”, + astu, „neka bude” (od as, „biti”): „neka je pozdrav (tebi)”.
    te
    “To you” (dative of tvam, “you”). The whole verse comes from the Devī Māhātmya (11.10), the great hymn to the Goddess.„Tebi” (dativ od tvam, „ti”). Cijeli stih je iz Devī Māhātmye (11.10), velikog hvalospjeva Božici.
    Oṃ
    The sacred syllable, the sound of the whole: beginning, middle and end in one breath. Every mantra opens and rests in it.Sveti slog, zvuk cjeline: početak, sredina i kraj u jednom dahu. Svaka mantra se njime otvara i u njemu počiva.
    Bhavānī
    “She who gives life”, from bhava, “being, becoming”: the Goddess as Śiva's wife, the mother of all that comes to be.„Ona koja daje život”, od bhava, „bivanje, nastajanje”: Božica kao Šivina supruga, majka svega što nastaje.
    Śaṅkarī
    The feminine of Śaṅkara, “the one who brings peace and good”, a name of Śiva: the Goddess as his gentle power.Ženski oblik od Śaṅkara, „onaj koji donosi mir i dobro”, ime Šive: Božica kao njegova blaga moć.
    Devī
    “The shining one”, the Goddess; devī is the feminine of deva, from div, “to shine”.„Sjajna”, Božica; devī je ženski oblik od deva, od div, „sjati”.
    Girijā
    “Born of the mountain”: Pārvatī, daughter of the Himālaya, Śiva's wife.„Rođena od planine”: Pārvatī, kći Himalaje, Šivina supruga.
    Hara
    “The one who takes away” (sorrow, fear, sin), a name of Śiva; here sung with the Goddess, who is his power: Hara Hara Devī.„Onaj koji odnosi” (tugu, strah, grijeh), ime Šive; ovdje pjevano s Božicom, koja je njegova moć: Hara Hara Devī.
    He
    “O!”: the call that opens a prayer or a name. He Mātā Kālī: “O Mother Kālī!”„O!”: zaziv kojim počinje molitva ili ime. He Mātā Kālī: „O Majko Kali!”
    Mātā
    “Mother”. The Goddess is called first of all as mother.„Majka”. Božicu se prije svega zaziva kao majku.
    Durgā
    “The one hard to reach”, the invincible: the Goddess as the warrior who defeats every demon, riding a lion.„Ona do koje je teško doći”, nepobjediva: Božica kao ratnica koja pobjeđuje svakog demona, jašući lava.
    namo namaḥ
    “Bow, bow again”: namaḥ is “a bow, salutation”, said twice for full devotion. Kālī Durgā namo namaḥ: “to Kālī and Durgā, again and again our bow.”„Naklon, opet naklon”: namaḥ je „naklon, pozdrav”, rečeno dvaput za puninu predanosti. Kālī Durgā namo namaḥ: „Kali i Durgi, iznova naš naklon.”
    Mā
    “Mother, Ma”: the short, tender word a child says. Kālī Mā, Durgā Mā: “Mother Kālī, Mother Durgā”.„Mama, majka”: kratka, nježna riječ koju kaže dijete. Kālī Mā, Durgā Mā: „Majko Kali, Majko Durga”.
    Jaya
    “Victory!”, “glory to”: Jaya Jaya, “glory, glory”.„Pobjeda!”, „slava”: Jaya Jaya, „slava, slava”.
    Jagadambe
    Vocative of Jagadambā: jagat, “the world, all that moves”, + ambā, “mother”: “O Mother of the world!”Vokativ od Jagadambā: jagat, „svijet, sve što se kreće”, + ambā, „majka”: „O Majko svijeta!”

    Sarasvati Maha Lakshmi

    toza Sarasvati and LakshmiSarasvati i Lakšmi

    Sarasvatī, Mahā Lakṣmī

    Sarasvati is the goddess of knowledge, speech and music, the patron of every singer; Lakshmi brings abundance, beauty and grace. The song begins with a soft voice alone before the band comes in.Sarasvati je božica znanja, govora i glazbe, zaštitnica svakog pjevača; Lakšmi donosi obilje, ljepotu i milost. Pjesma počinje tihim glasom samim, prije nego što uđe bend.

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (3)
    Sarasvatī
    Saras, “lake, flowing water”, + -vatī, “she who has”: “she who flows”. The goddess of knowledge, speech, music and the arts, and a sacred river of the Veda.Saras, „jezero, tekuća voda”, + -vatī, „ona koja ima”: „ona koja teče”. Božica znanja, govora, glazbe i umjetnosti, i sveta rijeka Veda.
    Mahā
    “Great”.„Velik, velika”.
    Lakṣmī
    From lakṣ, “to mark, to aim”: “the auspicious sign”, good fortune. The goddess of prosperity, beauty and abundance, wife of Vishnu.Od lakṣ, „označiti, ciljati”: „sretan znak”, blagostanje. Božica obilja, ljepote i napretka, Višnuova žena.

    Kailasha Vasa Maha Deva

    toza ShivaŠivu

    Kailāsa vāsa, Mahā Deva

    “Great God who dwells on Kailash.” Shiva sits in stillness on the sacred mountain in the Himalaya: the silence from which all the music comes.„Veliki Bože koji prebivaš na Kailašu.“ Šiva sjedi u tišini na svetoj planini na Himalaji: u tišini iz koje dolazi sva glazba.

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (4)
    Kailāsa
    Mount Kailash in Tibet, the snow peak where Shiva dwells.Planina Kailaš na Tibetu, snježni vrh na kojem prebiva Šiva.
    vāsa
    “Dwelling, abode” (from vas, “to live, to stay”). Kailāsa vāsa: “you who dwell on Kailash”.„Prebivalište, boravak” (od vas, „živjeti, boraviti”). Kailāsa vāsa: „ti koji prebivaš na Kailašu”.
    Mahā
    “Great”.„Velik”.
    Deva
    “God”, literally “the shining one” (from div, “to shine”). Mahā Deva: “the great God”, Shiva.„Bog”, doslovno „onaj koji sja” (od div, „sjati”). Mahā Deva: „veliki Bog”, Šiva.

    Uma Parvati Ananda Ma

    toza ParvatiParvati

    Umā, Pārvatī, Ānanda Mā

    Uma and Parvati are names of Shiva's beloved, the daughter of the mountain; Ānanda Mā is “mother of bliss”. After Shiva's song, his other half.Uma i Parvati imena su Šivine voljene, kćeri planine; Ānanda Mā je „majka blaženstva“. Nakon Šivine pjesme, njegova druga polovica.

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (4)
    Umā
    A name of Parvati. The old story reads it as u mā, “oh, do not!”, her mother's plea when the girl went to the forest for her austerities; it also means light and calm.Ime Parvati. Stara priča tumači ga kao u mā, „oh, nemoj!”, molbu njezine majke kad je djevojka otišla u šumu na pokoru; znači i svjetlost i mir.
    Pārvatī
    “Daughter of the mountain” (parvata, “mountain”): the daughter of the Himalaya, Shiva's wife, mother of Ganesha.„Kći planine” (parvata, „planina”): kći Himalaje, Šivina žena, Ganešina majka.
    Ānanda
    “Bliss, joy” (ā + nand, “to rejoice”); the joy that is our true nature.„Blaženstvo, radost” (ā + nand, „radovati se”); radost koja je naša prava narav.
    Mā
    “Mother”, the loving word of Hindi and Bengali. Ānanda Mā: “Mother of bliss”.„Majka”, nježna riječ hindskog i bengalskog. Ānanda Mā: „Majko blaženstva”.

    Rama Rama Jaya Raja Ram

    toza RamaRamu

    Three parts, each back to the first: Rama Rama Jaya Raja Ram · Sri Krishna Govinda · Sri Ram Jay RamTri dijela, svaki se vraća prvom: Rama Rama Jaya Raja Ram · Sri Krishna Govinda · Sri Ram Jay Ram

    Rāma Rāma jaya rājā Rām
    Rāma Rāma jaya Sītā Rām
    Śrī Kṛṣṇa Govinda Hare Murāre
    He Nātha Nārāyaṇa Vāsudeva
    Śrī Rām jaya Rām jaya jaya Rām
    Sītā Rām

    “Rama, Rama, victory to Rama the king; victory to Sita and Rama.” In the middle the song turns to Krishna: “Holy Krishna, Govinda, Hari, who defeated the demon Mura; O Lord, Narayana, son of Vasudeva”, and comes back to Rama with the old mantra Śrī Rām jaya Rām. Rama and Krishna are two faces of the same Vishnu, the one who keeps the world.„Rama, Rama, slava Rami kralju; slava Siti i Rami.“ U sredini se pjesma okreće Krišni: „Sveti Krišna, Govinda, Hari, koji je pobijedio demona Muru; o Gospodine, Narayana, sine Vasudeve“, i vraća se Rami starom mantrom Śrī Rām jaya Rām. Rama i Krišna dva su lica istog Višnua, onoga koji čuva svijet.

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (14)
    Rāma
    “He who delights, who gives joy” (from ram, “to delight, to rest”). Vishnu's seventh coming, the hero of the Rāmāyaṇa.„Onaj koji raduje, koji daje radost” (od ram, „radovati se, počivati”). Sedmi Višnuov dolazak, junak Rāmāyaṇe.
    jaya
    “Victory, glory to …!”, from ji, “to conquer”.„Pobjeda, slava …!”, od ji, „pobijediti”.
    rājā
    “King” (from rāj, “to rule, to shine”). Rājā Rām: “King Rama”.„Kralj” (od rāj, „vladati, sjati”). Rājā Rām: „kralj Rama”.
    Rām
    The same name as Rāma, spoken the Hindi way, without the last a.Isto ime kao Rāma, izgovoreno na hindski način, bez završnog a.
    Sītā
    Rama's wife, daughter of the earth; love and faithfulness. Sītā Rām: the two named together, as one.Ramina žena, kći zemlje; ljubav i vjernost. Sītā Rām: oboje imenovani zajedno, kao jedno.
    Śrī
    “Holy, radiant, beautiful”: a word of honour before a divine name.„Sveti, sjajni, lijepi“: riječ poštovanja ispred božanskog imena.
    Kṛṣṇa
    “The dark one, the all-attractive”: Vishnu born as the cowherd who plays the flute and speaks the Bhagavad Gita.„Tamni, svima privlačni“: Višnu rođen kao pastir koji svira frulu i izgovara Bhagavad Gitu.
    Govinda
    “The one who finds and protects the cows”: Krishna as the cowherd, protector of all that lives.„Onaj koji pronalazi i štiti krave“: Krišna kao pastir, zaštitnik svega živoga.
    Hare
    “O Hari!”: the one who takes away (sorrow); a name of Vishnu and Krishna, in the form used to call him.„O Hari!“: onaj koji odnosi (tugu); ime Višnua i Krišne, u obliku kojim ga se zaziva.
    Murāre
    “O enemy of Mura!”: Krishna, who defeated the demon Mura.„O neprijatelju Mure!“: Krišna, koji je pobijedio demona Muru.
    He
    “O!”: the call before a name.„O!“: zaziv ispred imena.
    Nātha
    “Lord, protector, refuge”.„Gospodin, zaštitnik, utočište“.
    Nārāyaṇa
    “The refuge of all beings”, or “the one who rests on the waters”: Vishnu.„Utočište svih bića“, ili „onaj koji počiva na vodama“: Višnu.
    Vāsudeva
    “Son of Vasudeva”, Krishna; also “the god who dwells in all”.„Sin Vasudeve“, Krišna; također „bog koji prebiva u svemu“.

    From the Brihadaranyaka Upanishad, one of the oldest prayers there is: “Lead me from the unreal to the real, from darkness to light, from death to immortality. Om, peace, peace, peace.” We always end with it.Iz Brihadaranyaka Upanišade, jedna od najstarijih molitvi: „Vodi me od nestvarnog k stvarnom, od tame k svjetlu, od smrti k besmrtnosti. Om, mir, mir, mir.“ Uvijek završavamo s njom.

    Dictionary · every wordRječnik · svaka riječ (10)
    asato
    Asataḥ, “from the unreal”: the ablative (the “from” case) of asat, non-being (a-, “not”, + sat, “being”).Asataḥ, „od nestvarnog”: ablativ (padež „od”) od asat, nebitak (a-, „ne”, + sat, „bitak”).
    mā
    “Me”, a short form of mām.„Mene”, kratki oblik od mām.
    sad
    Sat, “being, the real, the true”; it sounds sad before the g of gamaya.Sat, „bitak, stvarno, istinito”; ispred g u gamaya izgovara se sad.
    gamaya
    “Lead! Make go!”, the causative imperative of gam, “to go”. Asato mā sad gamaya: “lead me from the unreal to the real”.„Vodi! Učini da idem!”, kauzativni imperativ od gam, „ići”. Asato mā sad gamaya: „vodi me od nestvarnog k stvarnom”.
    tamaso
    Tamasaḥ, “from darkness” (tamas, “darkness, inertia”).Tamasaḥ, „od tame” (tamas, „tama, tromost”).
    jyotir
    Jyotiḥ, “light”.Jyotiḥ, „svjetlost”.
    mṛtyor
    Mṛtyoḥ, “from death” (mṛtyu, “death”).Mṛtyoḥ, „od smrti” (mṛtyu, „smrt”).
    amṛtaṁ
    A-, “not”, + mṛta, “dead”: deathlessness, immortality; also the nectar of the gods.A-, „ne”, + mṛta, „mrtav”: besmrtnost; i nektar bogova.
    Oṁ
    The sacred syllable, the sound of the whole of being, said at the start and the end of prayers.Sveti slog, zvuk cjelokupnog bitka, izgovara se na početku i na kraju molitve.
    śāntiḥ
    “Peace”. Said three times: peace in ourselves, peace with the world around us, peace from the powers above. The verse is from the Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣad (1.3.28).„Mir”. Izgovara se tri puta: mir u nama, mir sa svijetom oko nas, mir od viših sila. Stih je iz Bṛhadāraṇyaka Upaniṣade (1.3.28).

    One case. Ten minutes.Jedna kutija. Deset minuta.

    Everything the band needs to sound good and record every concert travels in one rolling rack: the camera tablet in the top drawer, the mixer under it, then the microphones, the cables and a drawer for everything else. Otvori, uključi, sviraj.

    How we keep it fastKako to ide brzo

    • One static setupJedna stalna postavaThe same ten channels every time, in the same order. The mixer remembers the mix (a stored scene), so a soundcheck is a check, not a new mix.Istih deset kanala svaki put, istim redom. Mikseta pamti miks (spremljena scena), pa je tonska proba provjera, a ne novi miks.
    • One stage box, one runJedan stage box, jedan vodThe stage box sits behind the band; everyone plugs in with a cable of 2 m at most. One run of four network cables, 10 m, goes to the mixer on the left of the stage (seen from the hall). No long cables between the players.Stage box stoji iza benda; svatko se uključuje kabelom od najviše 2 m. Jedan vod od četiri mrežna kabela, 10 m, ide do miksete na lijevoj strani pozornice (gledano iz dvorane). Nema dugih kabela među sviračima.
    • Mixed from a tabletMiks s tabletaThe CQ-12T has its own Wi-Fi: the mix is made walking around the hall with the CQ MixPad or CQ4You app.CQ-12T ima vlastiti Wi-Fi: miks se radi šetajući dvoranom s aplikacijom CQ MixPad ili CQ4You.
    • Every concert recordedSvaki koncert snimljenThe CQ-12T records all channels to its SD card. That is where the concerts on the Player come from.CQ-12T snima sve kanale na svoju SD karticu. Odatle dolaze koncerti u Playeru.

    The stagePozornica

    The stage box behind the band is four small Cat Snake boxes strapped together: three take the ten microphones and DI boxes (inputs 1–12), the fourth gives back the main L/R and two monitors. Four shielded network cables, tied into one, run 10 m to the CQ-12T on the left of the stage, seen from the hall. Each network cable carries four analogue channels, phantom power included; it is not a network, only the wires.Stage box iza benda čine četiri male Cat Snake kutije spojene zajedno: tri primaju deset mikrofona i DI kutija (ulazi 1–12), četvrta vraća glavni L/D i dva monitora. Četiri oklopljena mrežna kabela, vezana u jedan, idu 10 m do CQ-12T na lijevoj strani pozornice, gledano iz dvorane. Svaki mrežni kabel nosi četiri analogna kanala, uključujući phantom napajanje; to nije mreža, samo žice.
